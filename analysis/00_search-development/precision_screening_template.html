Precision screening

What this page is

A screening form for the precision samples of the search development plan, step 3, method item 10. It holds the 100 records drawn at random, with seed 20261112, from each database's load_<database>.csv: __DBS__. Each record shows its title and abstract, with the abstract's source.

How to screen

Screen each record on its title and abstract against the Criteria in materials/screening-guide.qmd, shown on the right. Record include, exclude with one exclusion code, or undecidable when the title and abstract don't allow a decision. A record first published after 2025 is excluded with date. The code other needs a note saying why.

Keys: 1 include, 2 exclude, 3 undecidable, a to g pick the exclusion code, ← → move, n write a note, Esc leave the note.

Saving and export

Decisions save in this browser as you go. Export CSV downloads one file per database, precision_sample_<database>.csv, with the same columns as the sample plus note. Put them in data/raw/search-development/ in place of the unscreened files, commit and push; notebook 00 then computes precision. Import loads exported files back in, to resume on another browser or after a new database's sample is added.

Criteria

A source is included when all of these hold:

  • It concerns the sharing or reuse of research data.
  • It reports factors that influence sharing or reuse, including barriers, facilitators, and governance mechanisms.
  • It concerns health research (tier 1), or it is a review or synthesis of research in other fields or across fields (tier 2).

Sources are excluded when they concern public health data, health systems data, or clinical care data used only for surveillance, service delivery, or patient care. Sources that concern these data reused for research are eligible.
